Disability stats

Did you know 1.3 billion1 people worldwide have a significant disability? That is almost the entire population of China! Did you know people with disabilities (PwD) often travel with companions? When you combine PwD, their friends, family, and caregivers, we have a market segment with an estimated $13 trillion2 in spending power. $95 billion3 of this annual spend can be attributed to travel!

(Sources: World Health Organization1,Forbes2, Expedia3)

A victory lap for digital accessibility: Unveiling the accessible video player

The Accessibility team joined forces with the One Hero Web (OHW) Brand pod to create a best-in-class accessible video player! This player unlocks access for anyone who cannot hear the video or see the video or does not speak English as their primary language through the following features:

  • Descriptive transcripts: Text versions for guests with deaf-blindness and cognitive disabilities.

  • Closed captions: Toggleable text for those who are deaf or hard of hearing.

  • Audio descriptions: Narrations for guests who are blind or have low vision.

  • Language Support: Multi-language support for non-native English speakers.

Customer impact

Kris Murner, a content designer outside Hilton, says, “Wow! This is truly amazing and we need it in the education space, like, last week.” Her 20-year-old son, who has Stargardt disease and is legally blind, said, “Wait, they made audio descriptions a toggleable feature? That’s so cool.”

Huge thanks to Ken Blasko, Adam Page, Regan Rusnak, Amy Huff, the OHW Brand pod, and our Brand marketing partners for bringing this accessible video player capability to life. To date, five videos are live: four tutorials for Hilton for Business and one brand video for Graduate by Hilton.

Contest time: Show us the power of alt text (and win a prize)!

If a picture is worth a thousand words, alternative (alt) text read by screen readers are the voice for them.

But how do we sum up an image in only a few words?

Consider the context. Does the image add information or is it decorative? Does it illustrate a feeling or emotion? Sometimes, AI generates alt text; does it accurately describe the image in the page`s context?

How to write alt text

Be descriptive, not literal. Avoid bland descriptions and aim for concise, vivid text. Alt text can also boost SEO. Learn more in our GAAD 2024 presentation on alt text.

Hilton and the Paralympics

While not an official sponsor of the Paralympics, Hilton nonetheless recognizes the value of the games and engaged in multiple activations in the support of the games.

Para-athlete panel discussion

Two Team USA Paralympians joined us for a virtual panel discussion at this year’s Global Accessibility Awareness Day (GAAD). They shared insights into the need for accessible travel experiences for athletes with disabilities, especially hotel gym workouts. Several athletes shared their stories, and attendees learned steps that hotels can take to enhance gym accessibility.
