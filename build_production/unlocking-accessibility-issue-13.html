Hilton Honors App Redesign Puts Accessibility Front and Center

In the past month, there have been 2.8 million bookings with a booking revenue of $841 million for the Hilton Honors App which is an 8% increase year over year. With more guests choosing the Hilton Honors app for booking rooms, the team has taken a bold step forward with the launch of Mobile 3.0 (M3). M3 is a complete redesign focused on accessibility, performance, and modern design practices.

M3 is more than a visual refresh. It’s a full-scale revamp of the legacy mobile architecture (M2), embracing reusable components, adding multi-language support to help reach a wider audience and be more hospitable, streamlined processes, and a design system that supports scalability and consistency. The redesign also introduced a new delivery culture, with cross-functional pods working collaboratively to accelerate development and improve quality.

Accessibility has been a cornerstone of M3 from day one. Accessibility analysts were embedded early in the design phase, contributing to both the design system components and the overall user experience. Accessibility analysts were also involved in the development and QA processes through consulting when components were created in the app, and through testing the final product. This proactive approach ensured that accessibility was a part of the foundations that made up M3 and in turn brought us into compliance with international laws and regulations.

Key accessibility enhancements include:

  • Improved screen reader support for better navigation
  • Device-controlled text sizing for flexible readability
  • Enhanced color contrast for visual clarity
  • Larger touch targets to support motor accessibility
  • Simplified content and clear choices for cognitive ease

Testing for accessibility is ongoing throughout development. The team conducts:

  • Snapshot testing with screen reader outputs
  • Keyboard navigation testing
  • Dark mode compatibility checks
  • QA-led screen reader testing
  • Large text testing to ensure layout adaptability

"M3 shows what’s possible through the collaboration and dedication of our mobile app team,” said Bart May, Senior Director of Mobile App Product. “From architecture to accessibility, we’ve rebuilt the app with performance, scalability, and inclusive design at the core — creating a faster, smarter, and more welcoming experience for every guest.”

This integrated approach to accessibility not only improves usability for all users but also reflects the team’s commitment to inclusive design. M3 is accessible, modern, and user-first.

Accessible Travel with AI: Smart Glasses in Hospitality

For millions of blind and low-vision travelers, AI glasses are more than tech—they’re a gateway to independence. As of 2025, more than 30 companies have launched AI-powered smart glasses, spanning from global tech giants to niche innovators.

Leading names like Apple, Meta (Ray-Ban & Oakley), Google, and Amazon dominate the mainstream market, while a growing number of specialized startups are focusing on accessibility-driven solutions. In this space, Envision, Aira, and Meta’s collaboration with Be My Eyes stand out as key players, delivering transformative tools for blind and low-vision users.

Accessibility-driven AI glasses are wearable devices equipped with cameras, microphones, and AI software that assist users with:

  • Object recognition
  • Text reading
  • Navigation
  • Facial recognition
  • Scene description

Hilton has partnered with Be My Eyes, a platform that connects blind and low-vision individuals with volunteers and company representatives for real-time visual assistance. Through this partnership, HRCC agents have been actively supporting guests who reach out via the Be My Eyes app.

Agents have reported interactions involving AI-powered tools, including AI glasses. These technologies are being used by guests to enhance their independence and help them navigate hotel environments more effectively.

Top Guest Inquiries via Be My Eyes:

  1. Identifying Room Features:
    • Soap dispensers
    • Electrical outlets
    • Microwave buttons
    • Thermostat controls
  2. Technical Assistance:
    • Turning lights on/off
    • Operating room appliances
  3. Navigation Guidance:
    • Directions to elevators
    • Locating guest rooms

As AI glasses continue to evolve, Hilton remains committed to creating inclusive experiences for every guest. Stay tuned for more innovations in accessible travel.
