Empowering everyone: Hilton’s inward facing commitment to accessibility

At Hilton, we prioritize accessibility in everything we do to ensure all Team Members can perform their job tasks efficiently, regardless of ability. Thanks to the robust frameworks and processes developed by our Accessibility Team, Hilton is now among a select group of large companies that have advanced to the point where we can focus on ensuring that all internal applications used by our corporate and property Team Members are accessible.

Prodigy - inclusive user interface for team members

Prodigy is the go-to platform for managing add-ons rates and inventory settings, currently used by 2,000 hotels and 4,000 team members. With the help and guidance of their accessibility champions, the Prodigy team has made accessibility a top priority at every stage of their development process.

The Prodigy Component Library is fully accessible, enabling the UX team to create completely accessible user interfaces. The Storybook components used by the development team are coded with accessibility in mind. The EQAC team is trained to navigate assistive technologies like JAWS and regularly conducts accessibility audits on existing features to drive ongoing improvements. Additionally, the development and EQAC teams are trained in accessible coding practices and testing methods to ensure continuous enhancement of accessibility standards.

Zsuzasa Grexa, Sr. Software Engineer on Prodigy, emphasizes that "Working with Dave and Anthony on Prodigy brought to light often-overlooked aspects of accessibility, showing me how crucial every detail can be. From writing meaningful labels, creating accessible tables to managing focus, dynamic content and interactive components, they highlighted areas I hadn’t previously considered and helped me to deliver a more inclusive experience."

Hilton's focus on internal accessibility sets a high standard, aligning with the Team Member Promise to create the best, most inclusive home.

A11y team's 2024 successes

As we welcome the New Year, we’re thrilled to celebrate the A11y team's achievements in 2024! In addition to the launch of this newsletter, here are a few highlights:

  • Global Accessibility Awareness Day (GAAD): Our GAAD event spanned three days, from May 14 to 16, and was held at six global corporate offices. It featured 13 virtual sessions and 9 onsite events, attracting close to 1500 participants. The keynote speaker was Joe Devon, founder of the GAAD Foundation.
  • CSUN Assistive Technology Conference:Nearly 40 Hilton Team Members attended the conference, absorbing approximately 670 total hours focused on disability and accessibility training. The A11y team presented about their collaborative approach to building the accessible video player. Additionally, we’ve been selected to present again next year!
  • Digital Compliance: As part of our overall automated compliance monitoring efforts, we are thrilled to report that we are ending the year with a compliance score of 91% for our One Hero Web and Curated platforms and increased our number of pages scanned by 32%. While our compliance scores fluctuate throughout the year as new features (e.g. Hilton for Business) and web content (e.g. SLH, Graduate, etc.) are released this represents an overall increase of 3% for all accessibility standards that can be tested using our enterprise automated scanning tool called AxeMonitor. Ending 2024, we added A11y Acceptance Criteria to over 690 user stories. 547 A11y bugs were opened and over 50% of those have been resolved.
  • Comprehensive training: We delivered 2000+ hours of accessibility training. Connect with us for accessibility training like how to create accessible PowerPoints, emails, meetings and events and more. If you’re new, we also provide an introduction to accessibility.
  • Tech innovations:
    • Video Player: Our new accessible video player is best-in-class, featuring captions, audio descriptions, and descriptive transcripts that are crucial for everyone to be able to enjoy video content.
    • Connected Room: TV's now include a screen reader enabling our blind and low-vision guests access to the personalized in-room experience.
    • Hilton Honors: Our app received a significant facelift and now supports text enlargement and dark mode will be coming soon. These improvements increase usability by giving the user control of how they prefer to view content on their device.
  • 3rd Party Vendor Accessibility Program: We established a Third-Party Accessible Vendor program and policy. This will ensure all procured technologies, including software, hardware, and digital content, are accessible to everyone, including those with disabilities.
  • Partnerships:
    • Be My Eyes: Hilton partnered with the Be My Eyes app, integrating AI-powered virtual assistance and direct support from our specially trained Hilton Reservation Customer Care (HRCC) agents to support blind and low-vision guests!
    • 3Play Media: A lot of rich, video content no longer simply isn't available to the full audience - and it enables many stakeholders to "add on" accessibility without a significant lift from the A11y team.
    • Grackle Docs: This partnership means that hotel owners who procure PDF flyers or menus can submit them, pay for remediation, and within a short period of time have materials that were not produced with accessibility added to their sites.

Looking ahead to 2025, we are excited to expand our accessibility initiatives to further enhance the guest experience.
