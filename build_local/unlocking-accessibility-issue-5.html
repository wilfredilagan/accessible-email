New program supports travelers requiring special assistance

In December 2023, American Express Global Business Travel (Amex GBT), the world’s leading B2B travel platform, introduced Travelers Requiring Special Assistance (TRSA) a global accessibility program for business travelers with apparent and non-apparent disabilities. The program aims to make business travel more comfortable and accessible for team members with disabilities, combining service-based solutions, advocacy, and technology.

Accessibility challenges

Accessibility is a significant obstacle for an estimated 1.3 billion people globally. Research by Amex GBT and Harvard Business Review Analytic Services shows that business travel is crucial for corporate growth, with 84% of respondents recognizing the value of in-person meetings. According to a 2022 research report from MMGY Global, a travel and tourism integrated marketing agency, lodging and transportation accessibility are the biggest barriers to travelers with mobility issues.

Accessibility Center of Excellence Involvement

In late 2023 and early 2024, Hilton's Accessibility Team and Linda Beaver, Global Travel Lead, explored a partnership with Amex GBT's TRSA program, and discussions about the program's benefits for team members with disabilities began. A focus group provided insights, leading to Hilton's decision to become an official partner.

Pilot Program Process

We are currently piloting the program with 4 team members. Following a successful completion, the TRSA program will be extended to all Hilton corporate employees with disabilities.

Key Highlights of the TRSA Program

  • Dedicated Support: End-to-end trip stewardship by a highly trained team.
  • Accessibility Solutions: Integration into booking and travel management for easier travel.
  • Enhanced Services: Complimentary hotel room upgrades, TSA PreCheck facilitation, and United Airlines lounge passes.
  • Personalized Assistance: Tailored support for various needs, ensuring every traveler feels valued.

This partnership sets a new standard in the business travel industry and demonstrates both Amex GBT's and Hilton's commitment to delivering distinguished service, making business travel more comfortable, confident, easy, and inclusive.

Making your documents accessible in Microsoft Office

Everyone has resolutions in the New Year, so this year, let’s resolve to make our documents more user-friendly for all! The Microsoft Office 365 Accessibility Checker can help. Think of it as your trusty sidekick, always ready to help you make documents (PowerPoint, Word, Excel, Outlook, and OneNote) friendly and accessible to everyone.

To use this helpful tool, open your document and head to the Review tab. Select Check Accessibility, and voilà! The Accessibility Checker will scan your document and present a list of issues and suggestions in the Accessibility pane. Each issue comes with a clear explanation and tips on how to fix it, making it easy to improve your document's accessibility.

Why is this important? Ensuring your documents are accessible means everyone, including people with disabilities, can read and interact with your content. It's all about creating an inclusive environment where everyone can participate and benefit. Give it a try! You will be surprised to learn how easy it is.
