Hilton leading the charge in accessible travel: Be My Eyes partnership

Hilton and Be My Eyes have joined forces to make hotel stays more reliable, friendly, and accessible for our guests who are blind or have low vision.

With the Be My Eyes app, guests can connect in real time to Hilton’s super-helpful customer care agents for guidance, making everything from check-in to breakfast a breeze. Our agents have been assisting guests with the TV, coffee maker, and thermostat most frequently. This partnership is rolled out across the U.S. and Canada, ensuring a warm and accessible welcome at Hilton’s portfolio of hotels.

“We believe that every guest – regardless of age, ability or any other factor – deserves a reliable and friendly travel experience and we are proud to partner with Be My Eyes to make that more of a reality for thousands of Be My Eyes users.”

- Mike Gathright, Senior Vice President, Customer Excellence and Innovation

To experience Be My Eyes in action, visit the Hilton YouTube channel and Matthew and Paul’s Instagram. To learn more about the Be My Eyes and Hilton partnership, visit Hilton Stories or our Be My Eyes SharePoint.

Recapping Disability Employment Awareness Month celebrations

National Disability Employment Awareness (NDEAM) is an annual observance in October to celebrate the value and talent of workers with disabilities and promote their access to good jobs. At Hilton, the Abilities Team Member Resource Group (ATMRG) has dropped the word “National” to give recognition to the amazing work this TMRG is doing around the globe at all our corporate offices. This year’s theme, Disabilities Don’t Discriminate, was filled with a cornucopia of intersectional events.

Maple Court hosts inclusive luncheon

A true feast of gratitude was hosted by ATMRG in partnership with UK Operations, Maple Court Workplace Services, and Encore Events. The event highlighted the skills and abilities of team members from Hilton’s supported employment partners: Aurora Foxes and Mission EmployAble. A generous $10,000 donation from Hilton and American Express Global Business Travel Pacesetters (top-performing travel agents) to the Down Syndrome Association’s WorkFit program was the gravy on top.

Be sure to check out the Disability Employment Awareness Month 2024 SharePoint and join the ATMRG to learn more about their impactful work.
