As part of our newsletter launch, the Accessibility team created a Microsoft Teams channel that as a centralized one-stop resource for getting your accessibility questions answered, receiving accessibility support, learning about Hilton’s accessibility efforts, participating in fun contests, and staying up to date with the latest accessible travel news.

Questions sub-channel

Use this sub-channel to post your accessibility questions. No topic is off limits. They can be technical about technical coding practices, requests for support on creating accessible documents or to simply get our support with training, consulting or disability inclusion efforts.

Unlocking Accessibility sub-channel

This sub-channel is an extension of this newsletter, where you’ll get the latest articles and news about accessibility at Hilton. This is where you can participate in our contests for a chance to where you can win awesome prizes!
