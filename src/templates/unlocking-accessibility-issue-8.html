Accessible PDFs ensure everyone, including people with disabilities, can read and interact with digital documents. PDFs are such a common digital format that there is an International Organization for Standardization (ISO) standard (ISO 14289-1) for PDF accessibility. Conforming with accessible standards is crucial, and GrackleDocs makes it easy.

Our partnership with GrackleDocs allows anyone at Hilton to submit a PDF for accessibility compliance testing and remediation, for a modest cost invoiced directly to their cost center. To take advantage of this service, upload your document to Grackle’s free analysis service to receive a letter grade. If the document receives less than an ‘A,’ Grackle’s remediation service can fix any issues and return the document, along with proof of accessibility compliance, making it ready for posting to the web or sharing more broadly. This service ensures compliance and broadens the reach of your documents.

Why accessible PDFs matter

  • Improved user experience: Web pages are the best and most accessible place to publish content, but we understand that PDFs are sometimes necessary. When you must publish a PDF, please take care to make it accessible.
  • Legal compliance: They meet requirements like the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA), Section 508, and the soon-enforced European Accessibility Act (EAA).
  • Broader reach: Accessible documents can be read by a wider audience, including those using assistive technologies.

GrackleDocs features

  • Automated accessibility checks: Quickly identify and fix issues.
  • User-friendly interface: Easy to use, even for non-experts.

Inclusive meetings are the secret sauce to a thriving, diverse workplace. They ensure that every voice, opinion, and idea is heard, creating a safe space where everyone feels comfortable and empowered to speak up – not just a select few. And guess what? Diverse companies consistently outperform their less diverse counterparts.

In advance of GAAD next month, we're excited to share how we're making each virtual presentation inclusive:

GAAD preparations:

  • Describe content: Presenters will verbally describe images, charts, and graphs in their slide decks. This helps everyone, especially those using screen readers, follow along.
  • Recording, transcription and captioning: Presenters will describe where to find the live captioning feature, ensuring everyone can participate fully. All sessions will also be recorded and transcribed so everyone can access all the content after the session.
  • Accessible slide decks: We provide accessible slide decks during and after the event.
  • PowerPoint live: We’ll be using the PowerPoint Live feature within Microsoft Teams to share slide decks so screen reader users can also access the content on the slides. It's a game-changer for accessibility!

Other tips for inclusive meetings:

  • Meeting invites: Include dial-in information and Microsoft Teams links in the body of your meeting invite, not just in the location field. This makes it easier for everyone to join.
  • Advance copies: If possible, provide presentation materials to all participants before the meeting.
  • Allow time: Some people take longer to express their ideas. Allow time for everyone to respond at their own speed.
  • Agenda and handouts: Briefly describe the agenda and handout materials. This helps everyone stay on track.

For more information about inclusive meetings, visit our Accessibility Center of Excellence SharePoint page on accessible virtual meetings. Let's make all our meetings accessible for everyone, using GAAD as our shining example. Together, we can create a more inclusive and diverse workplace! 🎉
