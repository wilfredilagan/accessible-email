In the ever-evolving world of digital experiences, accessibility isn’t just a checklist, it’s a commitment. At Hilton, the Quality Engineering (QE) team has taken this commitment to heart, embedding accessibility into every step of their development workflow. Thanks to the close collaboration with our accessibility team, Hilton has transformed its testing processes, culture, and ultimately, the guest experience across all 24 brands.

Accessibility testing at Hilton is not an afterthought. It’s seamlessly integrated into the QE workflow, with every ticket undergoing thorough accessibility checks right after functional testing. The scope spans from screen reader support, using JAWS for web, TalkBack for Android, and VoiceOver for iOS, to core usability features like keyboard navigation, zoom functionality, and semantic headings. It’s a bold statement: accessibility is no longer an add-on; it’s standard.

This shift didn’t happen overnight. According to a QE engineer, Racana Dixit, accessibility testing began back in 2014 with limited awareness and rudimentary checks. But over time, the teams cultivated deeper expertise, developed rigorous testing protocols, and streamlined priorities. Where there was once a massive backlog of accessibility defects, issues are now treated with the same urgency and importance as any other bugs. Automation tools like Cypress have also stepped in, helping identify visual contrast concerns early in development.

A standout feature of Hilton’s approach is the embedded partnership with the accessibility team. Each product pod includes an accessibility analyst who’s involved from the backlog refinement stage onward. This proactive approach ensures accessibility is considered before QE even begins testing. And when questions arise, QE testers receive direct support from experts on the accessibility team creating a seamless feedback loop.

New QE team members are trained with accessibility in mind, reinforcing the belief that inclusive design is everyone’s responsibility. The results speak for themselves: Hilton has not only eliminated its backlog but consistently delivers accessible digital experiences across web and mobile platforms.

Inclusion isn’t just a goal; it’s baked into the workflow. And with accessibility woven into Hilton’s engineering fabric, the future of travel looks more welcoming for everyone.
