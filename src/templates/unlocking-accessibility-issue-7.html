Autism, or Autism Spectrum Disorder, is a developmental condition that presents a spectrum of experiences, from challenges in social interaction and communication to remarkable talents in areas like mathematics, music, and art. Early diagnosis and intervention are crucial, acting as keys to unlocking the full potential of individuals with autism.

Autism Awareness Month is celebrated annually in April to foster understanding and acceptance.

This year’s theme, ‘Empowering Team Member Voices,’ seeks to amplify the unique perspectives and contributions of individuals with autism. Imagine a world where every voice is heard, every story is told, and every individual is celebrated for their unique view of the world.

Autism Awareness Month is a vibrant reminder of our ongoing efforts to support and celebrate the neurodiverse community. Through increased awareness, education, and advocacy, we can work together to create a more inclusive and understanding society.

The Abilities Team Member Resource Group (ATMRG) will host a panel of team members at 10 am CT April 17 during their bimonthly meeting. Team members who identify as being on the autism spectrum and caregivers for people with autism will share their stories.
