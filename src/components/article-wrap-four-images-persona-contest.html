User personas help keep the user at the center of the design process, leading to more intuitive and successful products. Our UX and Research partners have developed user personas that include disability characteristics. December’s contest showcases our partners’ personas.

Guest Personas Quotes

Ann and Bill Lawler

Ann and Bill are in their late 60s and early 70s, dealing with age-related vision loss and arthritis. They need accessible technology to help with their mobility and low vision.

1. "Reading text that's too light is a challenge. I need to be able to make it bigger to see it clearly."

Avery Hardworker

Avery is 40 years old with attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder, or ADHD. They prefer digital communication and need clear, distraction-free interfaces.

2. "I truly appreciate having a quiet room at the hotel when I feel overstimulated."

Millie Neal

Millie is 28 years old, deaf, and an early tech adopter. She needs accessible communications during her travels.

3. "I really hope the meeting I'm attending has a sign language interpreter. It would make such a difference for me."

Olive Kidman

Olive is 34 years old, blind, and travels with a guide dog. She needs accessible technology and assistance from hotel staff.

4. "I appreciate the friendly staff who assist me with breakfast each morning."
